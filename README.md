## Hi there 👋 I'm Yu-Shu

## What drives me

I can't quite explain where my curiosity about life comes from. I simply
want to understand how it works, and that curiosity keeps me asking
questions and learning. I also hope that what we discover can improve
people's quality of life.

My research has taken me from designing and synthesizing candidate
anticancer compounds to studying telomerase RNA and telomere biology.
These experiences have given me perspectives from chemistry and biology,
and I'm now developing computational skills to study biological systems.

I enjoy looking at questions from different angles and finding connections
between fields. I hope to bring these approaches together to investigate
health and disease and explore paths toward better treatments—so more
people can spend meaningful time with those they love.
