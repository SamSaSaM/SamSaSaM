## Hi there 👋 I'm Yu-Shu

## What drives me

I cannot quite explain where my curiosity about life comes from. I simply want to understand how living systems work, and that curiosity keeps me asking questions and learning. I also hope that what we discover can improve people’s quality of life.

My research has taken me from designing and synthesizing candidate anticancer compounds (organic, medicinal chemistry) to using dCas9-based tools to investigate gene regulation. In my doctoral research, I study telomerase in insect across function, purification, and biogenesis. In human, telomerase plays important roles in telomere maintenance, stem cell biology, and cancer, connecting many of the questions that interest me. Working across chemistry and biology has taught me to approach problems from different perspectives, and I am now developing computational skills to study biological systems.

I enjoy finding connections between fields and using them to ask new questions. I hope to bring these approaches together to investigate health and disease and explore paths toward better treatments, so more people can spend meaningful time with those they love.
